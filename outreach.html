Science beyond the lab

We believe research should speak to more than specialists. Here are some of the ways we have tried to bring microbial evolution to broader audiences.

Comic book

A comic book about phages

We collaborated with a comic book artist, Paweł Piechnik, to create an illustrated story about bacteriophages and why they are important for the society. The comic is aimed at everyone and no biology background is required. We hope you like it! Available in Polish and English.

Radio interview

Rafal's appearance on "OFF Czarek"

On 25 March, Rafal was a guest on the OFF Czarek programme on Radio TOK FM. The discussion explored ancient bacteria as a source of new antibiotics and the ongoing molecular “arms race” between bacteria and viruses, highlighting their potential for future biotechnology and infection control. The full interview can be accessed as a podcast here.

TV interview

Rafal's interview on TVP Nauka

On 25 November, Rafal was featured on Telewizja Polska (TVP Nauka) in the programme Nauka jest wszędzie ("Science Is Everywhere"). He discussed vaccines, their role in public health and the challenges posed by viral evolution. All episodes of TVP Nauka can be found under this link.
